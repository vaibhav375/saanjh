      How we know it works

      Measured on two full seasons of GK-2A images replayed on AWS. Methods were tuned on 2024 and tested on 2025, which the methods never saw.

      How this works

      Every 10 minutes the Korean GK-2A satellite images Punjab and Haryana. Saanjh finds fires in each image, follows their smoke with forecast winds and the height of the night-time air layer, and adds it up over where people live.

      What it doesn't do

      It works on a 2–4 km grid and never names farmers or plots. Small fires under about 10 MW are often too faint to see. Smoke levels show the farm-fire share only, not total pollution.

      Data

      GK-2A (KMA, via NOAA Open Data on AWS) · VIIRS active fires (NASA FIRMS) · ERA5 and forecasts (Open-Meteo) · WorldPop 2020 · geoBoundaries. Built on AWS Lambda, Step Functions, SQS, SNS, S3.
